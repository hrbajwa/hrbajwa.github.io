01 ABOUT

Research informed by biology, driven by data.

Across disciplines, a consistent question: why do infectious diseases emerge, spread, and persist?

My work sits at the intersection of veterinary science, parasitology, infectious disease epidemiology, and computational biology.

Starting with training in veterinary medicine and parasitology, I developed a research focus on population-level infectious disease patterns. My doctoral work advanced this perspective through national outbreak surveillance, spatial epidemiology, and quantitative analysis.

As a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Evolution, Ecology, and Behavior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, I investigate enteric pathogen evolution and transmission through genomic, epidemiological, and ecological evidence.

“

I aim to bridge pathogen-level mechanisms and population-level evidence to strengthen infectious disease surveillance and public health decision-making.
